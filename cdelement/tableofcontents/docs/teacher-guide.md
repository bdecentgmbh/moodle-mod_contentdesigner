# Table of contents element — Teacher guide

The Table of contents element lists the chapters of the activity with the learner's progress. It is shown
above the content, once per activity.

## Settings

- **Intro text**: a text above the list.
- **Call to action**: a button to start, resume or review the content.
- **Sticky table of contents**: stays at the top of the page, always or when scrolling up.
- Visibility of the table and of its sticky version: visible, hidden, or hidden on mobile.
