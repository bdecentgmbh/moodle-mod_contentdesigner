# Video Time element — Teacher guide

The Video Time element embeds a Video Time activity. Requires the plugin
[Video Time](https://moodle.org/plugins/mod_videotime).

## Settings

- **Course** and **Video Time activity**: an activity of this course, or of another course you can update.
- **Mandatory**: the elements after the video appear once the learner completed the Video Time activity (with
  sequential navigation).

Learners need access to the course of the video: enrolment, or guest access. Guest access with a password works once
the learner entered the password in their session.

When a course is copied or restored, the element points to the copied Video Time activity of the same course; videos
of other courses are kept on the same site.
