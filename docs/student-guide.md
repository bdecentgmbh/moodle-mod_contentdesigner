# Content Designer — Student guide

A Content Designer activity contains learning content in chapters. You work through the chapters one after the other.

## Working through the content

- The **progress bar** at the top shows the chapters of the activity; completed chapters are marked. Click a chapter
  in the progress bar to jump to it.
- At the end of a chapter, click **Mark as done** to complete it. The next chapter appears below.
- Some elements are **mandatory**, for example interactive H5P content. The elements after them appear once you
  completed them. A notice tells you when something has to be completed first.
- When you completed all chapters, the outro at the end of the content appears, often with buttons to go on, for
  example to the next activity.

Your progress is saved: when you come back, you continue where you left off.

Your teacher may show the content in **pages**: one chapter per page. Use **Next page** and **Previous page** to move
between them. *Next page* is available once you completed the chapter on the page (and its mandatory elements); the
outro is on the last page.

Depending on the settings of your teacher, chapters may also be completed automatically when you scroll to their end,
or the activity may be graded in attempts; the activity then shows your attempts and a button to start one.
